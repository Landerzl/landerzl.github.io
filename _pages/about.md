I'm a physicist working on experimental quantum sensing. At [Qubiz.team](https://qubiz.team) I build and operate a fiber-based optical quantum magnetometer, running CW-ODMR, pulsed Rabi and $$T_1$$ measurements, and I design microwave waveguides for hyperpolarization setups in Ansys HFSS and COMSOL.

Before that, I worked with low-temperature STM at [CIC nanoGUNE](https://www.nanogune.eu) in Prof. Nacho Pascual's group, where I characterized a cryogenic microwave antenna integrated into the STM junction as groundwork for ESR-STM.

I did my B.Sc. in Physics at the University of the Basque Country (EHU), 2022–2026, finishing first in my cohort (Extraordinary Degree Award). My bachelor's thesis, on _critical metrology_, looked at whether parameter estimation can gain precision near a quantum phase transition, using the superradiant transition of the quantum Rabi model.

I'm interested in quantum hardware: superconducting qubits, spin-based sensors, noise characterization, and the microwave engineering that connects them.
