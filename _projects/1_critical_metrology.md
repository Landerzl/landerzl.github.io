**Question.** Can quantum metrology gain from operating near a quantum phase transition?

**Approach.** I used the superradiant transition of the quantum Rabi model in the classical-oscillator limit as a test case. The analytic part relies on Schrieffer–Wolff and bosonic Bogoliubov transformations; the numerical part on dense diagonalization and numerical differentiation.

**Result.** The precision of parameter estimation diverges at the critical point in the static limit.

**Details.** Bachelor's thesis at the University of the Basque Country (EHU), Sep. 2025 – Jul. 2026, supervised by Iñigo Egusquiza and Jagoba Apellaniz, funded by an Ikasiker grant from the Basque Government. Graded 10/10 with Honors.
