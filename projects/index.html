Critical Metrology

Quantum Fisher information across the superradiant transition of the quantum Rabi model. Bachelor's thesis, 10/10 with Honors.

Molecular Architectonics

Nanographene spin chains and ESR-STM at CIC nanoGUNE. On-surface synthesis, STM spectroscopy, and spin-model numerics.

NV-center magnetometry

A fiber-based NV-center magnetometer at Qubiz.team, microwave waveguide design in HFSS, and open-system and DFT simulations of NV centers.

Bench-top NMR

A home-built NMR spectrometer aimed at portable ¹⁹F detection of PFAS. RF hardware and ML-based signal enhancement.

Superconducting resonator for STM

Designing a superconducting microwave resonator for a low-temperature STM, with Prof. Nacho Pascual's group. To be continued.
